RNAi Model

Overview

Lambert iGEM determined a system of deterministic Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) to model our RNA-interference (RNAi) reaction in Sf9 insect cells. RNAi can be summarized with the following steps:

  • siRNA synthesis: siRNA designs targeting three Varroa destructor salivary gland genes are ordered as DNA oligonucleotides and transcribed into RNA duplexes by in vitro transcription. The three most efficient are combined into a single multiplexed dsRNA construct.
  • Co-transfection: Sf9 cells are co-transfected with the psiCHECK-2 dual-luciferase reporter and design.
  • Knockdown: siRNA loads onto Argonaute-2 to form RNA-induced silencing complex(RISC), cleaving the Renilla transcript, which carries the three target sites in its 3′UTR
  • Detection: Luminescence is read with the Promega Dual-Glo system and knockdown appears as a drop in the Renilla to Firefly ratio relative to a non-targeting control.

Development

To assist in interpreting and validating RNAi reactions, we incorporated an ODE model to simulate the gene-silencing and correlate Renilla and firefly luciferase expression with siRNA dose and time elapsed. The model is intended to guide the wetlab in interpreting the luminescence output recorded.

Signaling Pathway

We used MATLAB’s SimBiology software to diagram each reaction using the Law of Mass Action and Michaelis Menten Enzyme Kinetics. The Mass Action equations are used to diagram the collision of reactants in a solution, which is needed for all the reactants in our reaction pathways. Michaelis Menten is to properly diagram catalytic enzyme kinetics when utilizing multiple ligand areas. The pathway begins with the introduction of a multiplexed dsRNA and Reporter construct into a system and ends with production of Renilla and Firefly luminescence.

Assumptions

The RNAi ODE model assumes rate constants as continuous, as Sf9 cultures are held at a constant 27 °C without external environmental factors that could alter them. Therefore, the model is able to isolate the silencing process to simulate knockdown of the Renilla reporter. The model also assumes that the initial quantities of siRNA, Argonaute-2, Dicer-2, and the psiCHECK-2 reporter are known, and that the cytoplasm behaves as a single well-mixed compartment. Additionally, the model assumes that synthetic 21-mer siRNA duplexes bypass Dicer-2 processing, that all three target sites sit within a single Renilla transcript, and that RISC cleaves that transcript at a constant rate regardless of which siRNA is loaded.
