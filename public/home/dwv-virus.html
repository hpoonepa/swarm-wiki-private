Deformed wing virus

A tiny shell of protein about 30 nanometres across, built from 60 identical units of three proteins. Inside it carries the virus's RNA.

  • VP1
  • VP2
  • VP3
  • P domain, a bump of VP3 on the surface

Real structure: capsid from PDB 5L8Q, repeated by its 60-fold icosahedral symmetry. Škubník et al., 2017, PNAS 114:3210. Colours follow that paper (VP1 blue, VP2 green, VP3 red, P domain magenta); a real virus is far smaller than a wavelength of light, so it has no colour of its own.
