2. RNAi

Now, the mite that spreads it.

RNA interference

RNAi

RNA interference.

RNA interference (RNAi) reduces gene activity by breaking down the messages cells use to make proteins. We’re targeting three salivary genes in Varroa mites, aiming to disrupt feeding.

  • Vd-CHIsal
  • Vd-CHIBIN
  • AV422

RNAi mechanism · schematic ↗
RNAi01 / 07

Double-stranded RNA.

The process begins with double-stranded RNA. Dicer approaches the strand.

Dicer cuts the strand into short siRNAs, which will guide RISC to a matching message.

Argonaute and other proteins come together in the RISC complex.

An siRNA is loaded into RISC, bringing the guide and the cutting protein together.

The two RNA strands separate. One stays with Argonaute as the guide.

The guide pairs with a matching sequence in the mite’s messenger RNA.

Guided by the siRNA, Argonaute cuts the matching mRNA. The released fragments are then broken down.
