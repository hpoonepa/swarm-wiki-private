# Modeling Instructional Series

## Overview

Lambert iGEM created a seven-video instructional series to help students and iGEM teams with limited modeling experience translate wetlab protocols into computational models. The series follows a four-stage workflow covering model development in MATLAB SimBiology and data visualization. Using two models from Lambert’s 2025 LANCET project, we walk viewers through individual protocols to demonstrate how wetlab workflows inform computational models and how model predictions help refine experimental designs. 

This connects modeling education directly to engineering by helping teams predict system behavior and refine, providing students with a practical starting point for applying modeling to their own projects. Overall, by making a modeling tool kit that others can learn from, adapt, and build upon, we are supporting broader participation in synthetic biology.

## Feedback Loop

Feedback from our first student viewers found the videos “lengthy” and difficult to apply the modeling concepts to their own projects, prompting us to shorten videos from 20–30 to 10–15 minutes, clarify modeling’s purpose, and use LANCET examples to demonstrate adaptable MATLAB workflows. Follow-up review showed greater engagement with the shorter videos, and students reported that they were more useful for applying modeling concepts. To further support students in applying these concepts to their own projects, we added a seventh video introducing a different type of model.
