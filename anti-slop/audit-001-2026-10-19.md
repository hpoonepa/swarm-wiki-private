# SWARM summary panel audit

Date: 2026-10-19. Mode: during, session override, with a final audit requested by the owner. The owner authorized the redesign and corrections during this session.

Scope: summary panels on Project Description and Implementation, their placement above the article and sidebar, title reflow, and the mobile Sections button focus color. This is a component audit, not a whole-wiki certification or a scientific fact check. Supplied article and summary prose was preserved in this revision.

## Direction and reasons

The Design Read declared before implementation was a research introduction for readers and judges, using SWARM's existing honeycomb and print-like typography. Dials: **ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1**.

- Color: warm ivory supports long-form reading, existing deep green carries the text, and a fine honey-colored outline relates the panel to the surrounding honeycomb.
- Layout: a single column, capped at 56rem, preserves the honeycomb on the right; the panel precedes both article and sidebar in source order.
- Typography: inherited site typography maintains continuity; only the opening paragraph of a multi-paragraph summary is larger, emphasizing the project's aim.
- Spacing: 20 to 36px padding and a 16px paragraph gap distinguish the summary; a 40 to 56px lower gap separates it from the detailed article.
- Container: the owner requested a box. Two angled corners echo the existing honeycomb without adding a second logo, icon, or branding strip.
- Labels: no visible editorial label remains. An accessible region name identifies the summary to assistive technology.
- Illustration: no new visual asset was created. The original honeycomb remains visible alongside the narrower panel.
- Motion and theme: the summary is static on the existing light reading surface, with no new animation, glow, shadow, or theme toggle.

## Resolved findings

1. MEDIUM, R-04/R-20/R-31: the repeated SWARM rail and three-hex icon competed with the page heading. Removed both.
2. MEDIUM, R-09/C-3: the visible summary label added editorial furniture the owner rejected. Removed it from both panels.
3. HIGH, R-03/C-4: clipping the container could hide enlarged text. Only decorative pseudo-elements now receive the corner clipping.
4. HIGH, R-03/R-35: the long Implementation heading overflowed at 200% text sizing. Added a shrinkable minimum width and emergency word wrapping.
5. HIGH, R-25/R-32: the mobile Sections button's gold focus outline had weak contrast on white. Changed the outline to the existing maroon token.
