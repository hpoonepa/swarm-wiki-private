# Reading typography refinement

Scope: Project Description and Implementation summary sizing and typography, shared article reading sizes, and selective emphasis in Project Description. Mode remains during, session override. This records the next refinement after audit 001; it does not certify the whole wiki or validate its scientific claims.

## Decision and evidence

The existing direction remains a research introduction using SWARM's honeycomb, with ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. The owner asked for a smaller box, even font sizing, and strategic bolding. The direction stated before this edit was to preserve the motif and headings, reduce width and padding, and use one reading size for summary and article.

There is no universally best font size. This implementation uses **18px desktop and 17px at widths up to 760px**, expressed in rem, with **1.65 line height**. Summary paragraphs now match the article, replacing the previous 22px opening paragraph, 18px secondary paragraph, and 17px desktop article text. The existing Space Grotesk font is retained.

[USWDS typography guidance](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/typography/) recommends an effective minimum of 16px for most body copy, at least 1.5 line height for longer passages, and approximately 66 characters as a useful line-length target. These are practical guidance, not proof that one size maximizes reading performance. The opening article paragraph measured 66 to 71 characters on its full desktop lines. The shorter summary measured 75 to 82 characters on full lines.

[W3C's visual presentation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/visual-presentation) informs the left alignment, reflow, and 200% text-resizing checks. This is not a claim of complete WCAG AAA compliance. [ONS guidance on online reading](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/content/writing-for-users/how-people-read-online) supports using emphasis sparingly and for a specific purpose. Bold text identifies the paired treatments, design requirements, selected facts, and named project work; it does not replace headings or highlight whole paragraphs.

Written visual reasons: the honey outline and ivory surface preserve the established motif; a 48rem width cap keeps the honeycomb clear; 16 to 24px padding reduces the box's footprint; equal font sizes remove accidental hierarchy; 650-weight emphasis creates a small number of scanning cues; the original heading scale separates sections; static rendering avoids distraction. No assets, new colors, or animations were added.

- PASS: at 390px, 200% text resizing rendered both summary and body at 34px without overflow or clipped summary text.
- PASS: overrides of 1.5 line height, .12em letter spacing, .16em word spacing, and 2em paragraph spacing caused no document overflow or summary clipping on either route.
- PASS: four desktop/mobile screenshots were inspected with Space Grotesk confirmed loaded. The final Description panel at 1440px measures 768 by 240px, compared with 896 by 276px before this revision using the same loaded font: approximately 14% narrower and 13% shorter.
- PASS: Description's Sections menu opens, Escape closes it and returns visible focus; citation 1 expands References and navigates to its target. No new controls were introduced.
- PASS: `git diff --check` completed without errors. Existing contrast colors are unchanged from audit 001: 13.46:1 for summary text and 12.48:1 for the maroon focus outline against white.

Validation was local browser inspection and geometry measurement, not a reading-speed study with participants. No new runtime script or network resource was added. Other browser engines and forced-colors rendering were not newly tested.
